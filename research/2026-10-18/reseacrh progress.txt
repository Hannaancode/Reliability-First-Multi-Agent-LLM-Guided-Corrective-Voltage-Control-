SIMPLE GLOSSARY
Training world: one fixed operating condition of the grid, including its particular
loads, generator conditions and outages. As a controller acts within that world,
it creates several states. Testing many bus/Q actions at those states creates many
labels. These states and labels share the world and are correlated; they are not
independent worlds. We have 31 finite historical training worlds plus a retained
invalid case. Recent development partitions use 23 optimization worlds and eight
historical held worlds. The historical held worlds are inspected development,
not an unused confirmation set.
PF: an actual nonlinear power-flow solve. An affine forecast is NOT a PF solve.
UV: undervoltage. OV: overvoltage. Clearing UV alone does not ensure normal-band
protection or full operational feasibility.
K4: whether four selected actions include the recorded best measured action.
Better K4 on saved labels is not yet a successful closed-loop recovery.
