# Focused closest-method review — 28 October 2026

This is a source-based addition to the thesis and comparator roster, not an exhaustive literature search or an executed benchmark. No new model, training or power-flow call was made. Repository commits and file metadata are saved in Pinned_Repository_Metadata.json. Preprints are labelled as such; an author README acceptance label is not independently verified publication status.

| Work and primary source | Relevance and limitation for this thesis |
|---|---|
| Chehade and Zhu, *Fine-Tuning LLMs to Generate Economical and Reliable Actions for the Power Grid*, arXiv:2602.15350v1, 17 February 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15350v1) | Uses supervised adaptation, AC-evaluated DPO and best-of-N for open-only corrective switching on IEEE 118-bus PSPS scenarios. Voltage results on a common-success subset do not establish unconditional full-cohort safety. The actuator and objective differ from HierGrid's positive shunt correction. This is direct prior art against claiming adaptation, preference learning or solver selection alone as a new contribution. The released workflow needs MATLAB/MATPOWER and hosted model training; it has not been executed here. |
| Yang et al., *Large Language Model as An Operator*, arXiv:2507.14800v2, revised 12 April 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14800v2) | Experience retrieval, generation and modification support day-ahead Volt/Var scheduling from forecasts. This differs from our immediate severe-undervoltage correction. The abstract and PDF titles differ in specificity; retain the versioned bibliographic title. The linked author repository at the pinned commit contains a supplementary PDF only. No executable controller or exact checkpoint was established by this intake; absence here is not a claim that no implementation exists elsewhere. |
| Yang et al., *Two-Stage Active Distribution Network Voltage Control via LLM-RL Collaboration*, arXiv:2602.21715v1, 25 February 2026. [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21715v1) | The abstract specifies an LLM selecting day-ahead tap/capacitor schedules and an RL agent controlling intra-day PV reactive power. It is relevant hybrid-control prior art. Only the primary abstract was verified in this intake; full implementation, data, weights and constraints remain unverified. Do not describe a hybrid LLM-plus-controller arrangement alone as novel or count this unavailable method as beaten. |
| Tripathi, Mohsenian-Rad and Raissi, *VeraGrid-Agent*, arXiv:2607.25155v1, 28 July 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25155v1) | Introduces 150 solver-grounded multiple-choice questions and tool/no-tool evaluation. This offers a public numerical-QA track for the RAG component, not a voltage-restoration score. The repository exposes frozen questions, circuit, solver reference and recorded traces. Its current runner uses external agent CLIs; a local SLM adapter would be a named new interface. Published scores are not our results. The reference solver and question-derived answers must remain outside the no-tool model context. |
| Subrahmanyam and Choudhary, *FLARE*, arXiv:2610.23251v1, 16 October 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23251v1) | Tests feeder restoration under incomplete observations, with evidence acquisition, switching and physical guards. This is relevant to later security and evidence-acquisition work. Its restoration task and voltage envelope differ from our strict 0.95–1.05 endpoint. The pinned repository explicitly omits the grid substrate and generated panels. Code availability therefore does not establish that its exact benchmark cases are available locally or reproducible now. |

## Decisions

1. Keep the current all-four native GraphAgent comparison first. Its saved action/state interface and exact-root evidence are more advanced than the newly found candidates. Report generator-voltage/existing-shunt actions and the native 136-operation allowance separately from our 25-operation added-shunt role; shared initial cases alone do not establish equal resources.
2. Admit VeraGrid-MCQ-150 as a **candidate** public QA dataset. Before any run, freeze the whole question set, license/access basis, solver revision, a label-hidden loader, local model identity/input/output contract, tool permissions, numerical controls and complete costs. Do not regenerate questions or use published test answers to tune prompts. Exact test labels were not read during this intake.
3. Keep FLARE on hold for exact substrate/panel availability. Do not substitute newly generated easier grids and call them the published test set.
4. Keep the two scheduling studies and PSPS switching study in the literature and admission matrix. Different actuators, information, horizons and objectives prevent a direct ranking from reported numbers. No paid API or new GPU allocation is justified by discovery alone.
5. Revise novelty language around the demonstrated combination, controlled evidence and limitations. Retrieval, reflection, adaptation, DPO, hybrid numerical/LLM control and an external safety guard are established ingredients. Our measured incremental benefit still requires matched controls and unused confirmation.

The reviewed 109-page thesis remains unchanged. T25/T31 are advanced by this source intake but stay open until the literature section and bibliography are integrated and visually checked. Five primary records and four repository commits are now documented; this is not a claim to have covered all published methods.
